Our Purpose

Rooted in Scripture. Sent to every nation.

SCRIPTUREi exists to place the Bible within reach of people everywhere while protecting the privacy and dignity of every reader.

01

Vision

To see the Word of God freely available to every nation, tribe, people, and language, anticipating the great multitude described in Revelation 7:9—people from every nation standing before the throne and before the Lamb.

SCRIPTUREi looks toward the biblical hope of the New Jerusalem, where God dwells with His people, wipes away every tear, and removes death, mourning, crying, and pain. The nations will walk by the light of God’s glory, the gates of the holy city will never be shut, and nothing unclean will enter it.

Our vision is for every person to encounter the Word that is a lamp to our feet and a light to our path, leading people to Jesus Christ and toward eternal life in God’s presence.

Revelation 7:9 · Revelation 21:1–4, 22–27 · Psalm 119:105

02

Mission

In obedience to the Great Commission, SCRIPTUREi exists to make disciples of all nations by providing free and private access to the Bible, proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and helping people learn and obey everything Christ commanded.

Because faith comes by hearing the Word of Christ, SCRIPTUREi seeks to place Scripture within reach of every person—without unnecessary accounts, advertising, behavioral profiling, or surveillance.

The Scriptures make people wise for salvation through faith in Jesus Christ and equip believers for every good work. Our hope is that people will hear the Word, believe in Jesus Christ, follow the Lamb, and receive the eternal promise of the New Jerusalem.

Matthew 28:18–20 · Romans 10:14–17 · 2 Timothy 3:15–17 · Revelation 22:1–5, 17
