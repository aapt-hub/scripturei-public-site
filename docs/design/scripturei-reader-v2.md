# SCRIPTUREi Reader V2 Design Specification

## Vision
The Reader is the core experience of SCRIPTUREi: a peaceful place where people come to read the Word of the Lord.

## Design Principles
- Scripture first
- Inclusive for all people
- No denominational labeling in the public experience
- No social media distractions inside the reading environment
- Technology serves the reading experience

## Reader Experience

### Visual Environment
- Use the SCRIPTUREi heaven/New Jerusalem background identity
- Increase background transparency approximately 40% to reduce distraction
- Maintain light, peace, and openness

### Reader Window
- Floating reading panel
- Expandable/resizable experience
- Opens approximately 85% of screen height
- Maximizes space for complete chapter reading
- Avoid excessive header areas

### Typography
Primary Bible text:
- Charis SIL recommended
- Large readable text
- Comfortable letter separation
- Kindle-like reading experience

### Controls
Minimal controls visible:
- Font size
- Audio
- Bookmark
- Share
- More options

### Share Philosophy
Share the Word, not social media.
Social platforms are available only through the device share action after user choice.

### Future Integration
Base66 can provide governed reference, identity, validation, and evidence layers while remaining subordinate to Scripture.
