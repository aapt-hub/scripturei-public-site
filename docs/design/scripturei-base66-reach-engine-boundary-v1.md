## Architecture Boundary

Base66 provides governed technical support:

- Scripture identity validation
- Edition identity
- Language/source evidence binding
- Hash integrity
- Provenance records

Base66 does NOT determine theological authority or replace Scripture.

## Reach Calculation Flow

Approved Scripture Edition
    -> Language Coverage Record
    -> Population Coverage Mapping
    -> Evidence Validation
    -> Reach Metric Update
    -> Public Website Display

## Fail Closed Rules

A language addition shall not change the public reach number unless:

- source identity exists
- evidence is complete
- validation passes
- rights status is approved or authorized
- change record is captured

## Vision

The metric represents people potentially served by available Scripture resources.

The purpose is to help communicate the continuing mission of making God's Word accessible to every nation and language.
