# SCRIPTUREi Reach Metric Control V1

## Mission Statement

"And this gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed throughout the whole world as a testimony to all nations..."

Matthew 24:14

## Dynamic Reach Display

The homepage mission section shall display a dynamic reach metric connected to Scripture availability data.

Example:

4.5 Billion+ reached

XX Billion still to reach

## Control Principle

The displayed number shall not be a static marketing value.

When new validated Bible languages/translations are added to SCRIPTUREi, the reach estimate shall update through a governed data process.

## Data Flow

New Scripture Edition
    -> Validation
    -> Rights Review
    -> Approved Language Coverage
    -> Reach Calculation Update
    -> Public Mission Display

## Governance

The metric must include:

- source data identity
- language/edition evidence
- calculation timestamp
- validation status
- change history

The number represents people who can potentially access Scripture resources, not a claim of individual faith response.

SCRIPTUREi serves the mission by making Scripture accessible, understandable, and shareable.
